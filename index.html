MoodGlow · V13.5
BEHIND THE EXPERIENCE

Music you can feel.
Light you can see.

MoodGlow is a small interactive emotional soundscape. Choose your present mood and the way you would like to feel, then listen to a procedurally arranged instrumental piece alongside a flowing light experience.

Current MoodSad, Stressed, Tired or Okay determines the shape and movement of the light. You can change it without restarting the music.
Desired FeelingUplifted, Calm or Refreshed determines the music arrangement, tempo and light color palette.
Light & musicThe glow follows beat and accent events calculated from the music's original 32-bar pattern, not live microphone input or audio-frequency analysis. The beat alignment is approximate for Strudel-driven tracks.

All three styles are procedurally arranged. Uplifted is sequenced with JavaScript and played using local WAV samples through Web Audio; Calm and Refreshed are played through Strudel. This is a creative experience, not an emotion detector or therapeutic treatment.
